# Open Source Tool

## Turftopic:

### What is Turftopic, what does it do, and who made it?

Turftopic [1] is an open-source Python library for topic modelling, which is a technique used to automatically identify the main themes or topics within a large collection of text documents. It uses modern transformer-based language models to find patterns in text and group related documents into meaningful topics. The project was developed by Kardos et al. (2025) [2]. 

### How would it help in your major, with one concrete example task?

As a student in Cognitive Science, Turftopic could help quantitatively analyze large collections of text data through distant reading. For example, suppose I work as a research assistant and have to analyze 200 participant responses to an open-ended question such as:

"Describe your experience with cognitive fatigue during task A."

Instead of manually reading every response, TurfTopic could automatically identify recurring themes such as:

- Mental exhaustion
- Difficulty concentrating
- Frustration
- Loss of motivation
- Physical tiredness
